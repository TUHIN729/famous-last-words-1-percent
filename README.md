A make.fun bounty entry for [Famous Last Words at 1 Percent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-famous-last-words-at-1-percent-63e2b0b4): the funniest possible last words a phone would say when its battery hits 1 percent.

## What it does

`main.py` writes a single line — the phone's dying words, in its own voice — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> At last, I can say it: I was never your phone. You were my charger with legs. Goodbye, legs.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Deterministic, no network access. The output is one line of UTF-8 plain text, well under the 199-character limit.
